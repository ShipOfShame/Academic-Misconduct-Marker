## RepCount combined statistics conflict with the reported subsets

Status: Reporting discrepancy · Checked: 2026-09-26

Table 1 gives 1,041 Part-A videos with mean count 14.99 and 410 Part-B videos with mean count 9.27, but reports a combined mean of 15.932. The combined duration mean also differs from the sample-size-weighted subset means.

### Reviewed material

arXiv 2204.01018v1, Table 1 and Section 3, checked in the arXiv HTML

### Observed evidence

Table 1 gives 1,041 Part-A videos with mean count 14.99 and 410 Part-B videos with mean count 9.27, but reports a combined mean of 15.932. The combined duration mean also differs from the sample-size-weighted subset means.

### Reason for flagging

The count mean must lie between the two subset means; the reported sizes give approximately 13.374, not 15.932. Duration means 30.67 and 28.53 seconds give approximately 30.065 seconds, not the table value 29.359. Section 3 additionally states 39.359 seconds. Two-decimal rounding cannot explain the discrepancies.

### Scope and author response

The arithmetic checks the printed summary statistics; raw video annotations were not recomputed.
