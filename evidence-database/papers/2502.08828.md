## The continuous-search characterization excludes a cited generative method

Status: Reporting discrepancy · Checked: 2026-09-26

Sections 5 and 7 contrast discrete RL search with continuous optimization by generative methods. Yet Section 4.2 includes Zhang et al. (2024b), whose original method generates discrete feature operations and selects and expands nodes with Monte Carlo Tree Search.

### Reviewed material

arXiv 2502.08828v2, Sections 4.2, 5 and 7; cited arXiv 2406.03505v1, Section 3.3.

### Observed evidence

Sections 5 and 7 contrast discrete RL search with continuous optimization by generative methods. Yet Section 4.2 includes Zhang et al. (2024b), whose original method generates discrete feature operations and selects and expands nodes with Monte Carlo Tree Search.

### Reason for flagging

A continuous latent-search property of one method family is generalized to a category that also contains discrete LLM tree search. Internal neural embeddings do not make the outer feature-search algorithm continuous.

### Scope and author response

The counterexample is present in the cited paper v1 from 2024, before this survey v2.
