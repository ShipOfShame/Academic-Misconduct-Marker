## Figure 6 inconsistently labels the accuracy metric

Status: Reporting discrepancy · Checked: 2026-09-26

The Qwen values in v4 Figure 6 correspond to question-level accuracy in the author’s issue reply, while the caption describes a percentage of individual test cases. The plotted metric and caption describe different quantities. Deliberate misrepresentation is not inferred.

### Reviewed material

arXiv 2508.09125v4 Figure 6 and the metric definitions in the issue #2 author reply.

### Observed evidence

The reply distinguishes case-level accuracies of 66.76% / 75.83% from question-level accuracies of 26.47% / 38.24% for Qwen3-8B / Qwen3-32B. Figure 6 shows 26.5% / 38.2%, matching question-level accuracy after rounding, while its caption describes the percentage of individual test cases.

### Reason for flagging

Passing every test case for a question is a different event from passing an individual case. Labeling question-level values as case-level accuracy misstates what the plotted performance measures.

### Scope and author response

The evidence supports a caption/metric-label error, not a claim that the plotted numbers were fabricated. The v4 PDF labels itself COLM 2026; the final venue archive was not independently obtained.
