# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Current release of Academic Misconduct Marker.

- Author and paper markers for arXiv and Google Scholar, with individual identity matching and linked evidence.
- Accessible hover cards, local database import and export, and marker visibility settings.
- Separate English and Chinese evidence websites and READMEs.
- Database-derived statistics and a dated publication inventory.
- Source-linked paper records, identity audits, and configurable review-priority scoring.
- Reproducible extension packages with SHA-256 checksums and publication privacy checks.
